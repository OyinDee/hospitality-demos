01 — Our story

A kitchen built
around the fire.

We opened in 2019 with one grill, a stack of oak, and a stubborn belief that the best meals are the ones that take their time.

Everything that leaves our kitchen meets live flame. Whole fish, dry-aged ribeye, vegetables from a handful of farms within a hundred miles. The menu shifts with what the season gives us, and the wine list follows the same rule: small growers, honest bottles, nothing that needs explaining.

The room is dark, warm and deliberately unhurried. Come for dinner, stay for one more glass.
